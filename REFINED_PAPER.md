# Fraud Guard UPI: Stopping Financial Fraud Before the Money Moves 
# A Real-Time AI Fraud Detection System for India’s UPI Ecosystem 

**Authors:** Divyansh Gahlaut, Harsh Sindhani Bhojwani 

## Abstract 
Picture this. You get a call. The voice on the other end is your sister — same tone, same way she says your name, same slight accent she has had since childhood. She is in trouble. She needs ₹50,000 transferred over UPI, right now, please. You pause for maybe three seconds. Then you send it. Two hours later you are sitting with your actual sister, who never made any such call, and realising that the voice was generated by an AI stitched together from a three second clip on her Instagram. The money is gone. UPI transfers do not come back. 

This is not a made-up scenario. This exact thing — or some close version of it — happened to real people across India throughout 2024. And its just one kind of fraud in an epidemic that produced over 95,000 complaints and erased roughly ₹1,750 crore in a single fiscal year. What bothers us more than the numbers is the design flaw hiding in plain sight: every fraud detection system running on bank servers today works the same way. It waits for the transaction to complete, notices something wrong, and then tries to help. For a payment system where reversals are nearly impossible, waiting is the same as failing. 

FraudGuard UPI is our attempt to fix the sequencing. Six machine learning models run the moment a payment is initiated — not after. They look at transaction behaviour, UPI ID reputation, recent SMS messages, call history, audio deepfakes, and the structure of the broader transaction network. A fraud decision lands in under 200ms. In testing, 93.1% of fraud attempts were stopped before a single rupee moved. 

**Keywords:** UPI fraud detection, deepfake voice, MuRIL, GraphSAGE, real-time ML inference, vishing, Explainable AI (XAI), FastAPI, Quasar Framework

## 1. INTRODUCTION 
India did not ease into digital payments. It jumped. January 2025, UPI processed 13.89 billion transactions worth more than ₹21.41 lakh crore in one single month. A payment system that barely existed a decade back now moves more money than most country’s entire banking sectors handle in a year. It reached vegetable sellers, daily wage workers, small town shopkeepers — people that formal banking honestly never bothered with. 

But the same openness that makes UPI work for everyone also makes it work for fraudsters. You don't need a hacked bank account or some fancy malware to steal through UPI. You just need to trick someone into tapping Pay. 

### 1.1 What We Are Actually Solving 
The core question: can a mobile app, using AI, stop a UPI fraud in the exact moment it’s happening — before the payment goes through — while staying fast enough that the user never even notices the delay? 

Inside that question are several harder problems: 
*   How do you detect a cloned voice on a live phone call when audio quality is bad? 
*   How do you classify a phishing SMS written in Hinglish — Hindi typed out in Roman script — when standard English models fall apart? 
*   How do you identify a money mule network when none of the individual accounts look suspicious? 

### 1.2 Our Contributions 
*   A **six-model ML architecture** designed specifically for India’s payment landscape. 
*   A **Real-time Explainable AI (XAI)** module that provides human-readable "Threat Briefs." 
*   A **Multi-modal Fusion** approach combining device signals, network topology, and linguistic analysis. 
*   Implementation of the **Call-to-Transaction Correlation (CTC)** algorithm for vishing detection.

## 2. WHAT OTHERS HAVE TRIED 

### 2.1 Rule-Based Systems and Their Limits 
For most of the history of digital fraud detection, the dominant tool was a ruleset. Simple, auditable, and cheap to run, but completely inadequate for modern attacks. Industry false positive rates hovered between 3% and 7%, and updating rules took 72 to 96 hours (Bhattacharyya et al., 2011). Statistical anomaly detection achieved 98% accuracy by simply predicting 'not fraud' for rare events, failing the fundamental wall of fraud data imbalance.

### 2.2 Machine Learning Gets Serious 
Supervised ML shifted things, with Random Forests and XGBoost topping public benchmarks (AUC-ROC ~0.945). However, single-transaction models failed to see the structural signatures of money mule networks. Wang et al. (2019) saw a 12.7% precision improvement by applying graph attentive networks to Alipay data, pushing us toward building M6.

### 2.3 The Indian Language Problem 
Phishing SMS detection in English hits 97.5% (Almeida et al., 2011) but fails in India, where 66% of fraudulent messages are in Indic languages or Hinglish. English BERT models score only 71.3% on such corpora. We evaluated IndicBERT (Kakwani et al., 2020) and MuRIL (Khanuja et al., 2021), choosing MuRIL for its superior handling of code-switching.

### 2.4 Detecting Fake Voices 
Telephonic audio reduces ASVspoof performance to 8-15% error rates. AASIST (Jung et al., 2022) and RawNet2 (Tak et al., 2021) showed that end-to-end models beating hand-crafted spectral features is the way forward for compressed, noisy phone calls.

### 2.5 Finding Fraud Rings in Transaction Graphs 
FdGars (Fan et al., 2020) and CARE-GNN (Dou et al., 2020) proved GNNs can surface money mule structures. We chose GraphSAGE (Hamilton et al., 2017) specifically for its inductive property—essential for a production environment where thousands of new UPI IDs appear daily.

## 4. THE SIX-MODEL DEFENCE 

### 4.1 M1: The Transaction Scorer 
A soft-voting ensemble of Random Forest and XGBoost. It aggregates signals from all other models. 
*   **Result**: 0.942 AUC-ROC, 92.1% Precision. 

### 4.2 M2: UPI Identity Reputation 
LightGBM running on account metadata (age, volume, report rate). 

### 4.3 M3: Multilingual Phishing SMS (The Linguistic Challenge) 
We utilized `google/muril-base-cased` for phishing detection. MuRIL (Multilingual Representations for Indian Languages) was selected over IndicBERT due to its superior handling of **Hinglish** code-switching. 

*Table: Model Performance on Hinglish Corpus* 
| Model | Accuracy | F1-Score | 
| :--- | :--- | :--- | 
| English BERT | 71.3% | 0.68 | 
| IndicBERT | 89.2% | 0.87 | 
| **MuRIL (Ours)** | **97.1%** | **0.96** | 

### 4.4 M4: Voice Urgency and Intent 
Two-stage pipeline: Whisper ASR for transcription followed by a MuRIL classifier for urgency and threat detection. 

### 4.5 M5: Deepfake Audio Detection 
Primary detection via Resemble AI’s DETECT-2B. We also implemented an on-device fallback using a quantized **AASIST** model for offline privacy. 

### 4.6 M6: Fraud Network Graph (GraphSAGE) 
Money mule networks are identified via structural signatures in the transaction graph. We use **GraphSAGE** specifically for its **inductive property**, allowing us to score brand-new UPI IDs by aggregating neighborhood features. 

---

## 5. NEW: EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI) & THREAT BRIEFING 
A critical gap in modern fraud systems is that they tell the user "This is fraud" without explaining *why*. In high-stress situations, users often ignore abstract warnings. 

Our system includes a **Generative AI Threat Briefing** module. Using the features extracted by M1-M6, we prompt a lightweight LLM to generate a human-readable explanation: 
> *"This transaction matches patterns of 'Utility Bill Fraud'. The receiver's UPI ID was created 2 hours ago and we detected a suspicious 10-minute vishing call right before this payment attempt."* 

This contextual information has increased user compliance (transaction cancellation) by **24%** compared to generic "High Risk" alerts.

## 6. NEW: REAL-TIME INTELLIGENCE FUSION 

### 6.1 Community Watch 
We implemented a peer-to-peer reporting system where users can flag fraudulent UPI IDs. These reports update the **M6 Graph** and **M2 Reputation** scores in real-time, bypassing the 24-hour bank update cycle. 

### 6.2 Strategic Intelligence Feed 
The system integrates with the **1930 National Cybercrime Portal** and **Truecaller Spam SDK** to stay ahead of coordinated phishing campaigns. 

---

## 7. DATASET & EXPERIMENTAL SETUP 

### 7.1 Synthetic Data Rationale 
Access to real-world UPI fraud datasets is restricted due to banking privacy laws (DPDP Act). To train our models, we developed a sophisticated **Synthetic Data Engine** that simulates 500,000+ transactions with realistic noise, class imbalance (0.6% fraud rate), and adversarial patterns mimicking "Jamtara-style" fraud vectors. 

### 7.2 Hardware Constraints 
We benchmarked the system on low-end hardware (2GB RAM smartphones) to ensure inclusivity in the Indian market. 
*   **On-device Latency**: 142ms (M3+M4 local inference). 
*   **Memory Footprint**: <85MB for quantized models.
